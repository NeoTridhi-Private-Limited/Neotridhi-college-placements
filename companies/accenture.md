## Several nets, one trial

Think of a cricket team trial with several nets. One net checks fitness (cognitive), one checks batting technique (technical), one checks
match play (coding), and one checks how you talk to the team (communication). Prep sites report that the cognitive and technical net is
where most people are dropped. The rest decide your level.

```
Apply (campus cell or Accenture careers) --> Cognitive + technical assessment --> Coding assessment --> Communication assessment --> Interview --> Offer
```

From Accenture's official "journey to Accenture" page: **external AI tools are not permitted** in the technical assessment, and if you fail
an assessment you must wait **at least 90 days** before retaking it.

## Campus assessment stages (commonly reported; varies by drive)

| Stage | Approx. content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive assessment | Analytical reasoning, abstract reasoning (shapes, series), problem solving, critical reasoning | Often reported as the main elimination stage |
| Technical assessment | Pseudocode, programming basics, OOP, networking, cloud, security, MS Office | MCQs |
| Coding | About 2 problems in about 45 minutes | Function-style problems |
| Communication | Spoken and written English (read aloud, repeat, listen, grammar) | AI-graded, reported |
| Interview | Technical + HR, often one panel | Project, basics, attitude |

## Try 5 questions (solutions open)

All questions are original, written in the style of the Accenture assessments, with new numbers and wording.

**1. (Cognitive: probability)** A bag has 5 red and 7 blue pens. Two pens are taken out together, at random.
What is the probability that both are red? (a) 5/33 (b) 25/144 (c) 5/12 (d) 1/6

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Ways to choose 2 red = C(5,2) = 10. Ways to choose any 2 = C(12,2) = 66.
Probability = 10/66 = **(a) 5/33**.
Trap: 25/144 = (5/12) x (5/12) assumes the first pen is put back. It is not.

</details>

**2. (Cognitive: time and work)** Kiran can finish a website in 15 days and Lalitha in 20 days. They work together for 4 days, then Kiran leaves.
How many more days does Lalitha take to finish? (a) 10 (b) 10 2/3 (c) 11 1/3 (d) 12

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Together per day = 1/15 + 1/20 = 4/60 + 3/60 = 7/60. In 4 days: 28/60 of the work.
Left = 32/60. Lalitha does 1/20 = 3/60 per day. Days = 32/3 = **(b) 10 2/3 days**.

</details>

**3. (Reasoning: critical reasoning)** Statement: "Last month a city metro cut every ticket price by 20%. The number of daily riders rose by 30%."
Conclusions: I. The price cut was the only reason riders increased. II. Daily ticket income went up.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

I: the statement does not rule out other reasons (a new line, exams, festivals). "Only reason" is too strong. Does not follow.
II: income = price x riders = 0.8 x 1.3 = 1.04 of the old income, a 4% rise. Follows.
Answer **(b) Only II follows**.

</details>

**4. (Communication: phrasal verbs)** "The manager asked us to look ___ the error before the client call."
(a) after (b) into (c) for (d) up

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

"Look into" = investigate. Answer **(b) into**. "Look after" = take care of; "look for" = search; "look up" = find in a list or dictionary.

</details>

**5. (Pseudocode: while-loop trace)** What is printed?

```
integer x = 10, y = 3
while (x > 0)
    x = x - y
    y = y + 1
end while
print x, y
```
(a) 0 6 (b) -2 6 (c) 3 5 (d) -2 5

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

| Step | x | y |
|---|---|---|
| start | 10 | 3 |
| 1 | 7 | 4 |
| 2 | 3 | 5 |
| 3 | -2 | 6 |

x = -2 is not > 0, so the loop stops. Output **(b) -2 6**. (We ran the same steps in Python and got -2 6.)

</details>

## Preview: Non-IT and MBA recruiters

### The idea in plain words

An IT test asks, "Can you build it?" These recruiters ask, "Can you think about money, customers and data?"
A kirana shop owner deciding how much stock to buy before Diwali is doing a mini case interview in his head.

### Worked example: an official practice case

**Official sample, source: [Bain & Company, Coffee Shop Co. practice case](https://www.bain.com/careers/hiring-process/interviewing/coffee-case-study/)**

> "How much coffee does she need to sell to break even in the first year?"

The case gives these figures (we list the data in our own words): price per coffee £3; cost per cup £1; one-time cost to open the shop
£245,610; cost to run the shop each year £163,740. (The case is set in Cambridge, UK. Earlier in the case it assumes a population of 100,000,
each drinking 1 cup a day.)

**Our worked solution**

1. First-year cost = opening cost + running cost = 245,610 + 163,740 = **£409,350**.
2. Margin per cup = price - cost per cup = 3 - 1 = **£2**.
3. Cups to break even = 409,350 / 2 = **204,675 cups** in the first year.
4. Per day: 204,675 / 365 = **about 561 cups a day** (560.75).
5. Sense check: the town drinks 100,000 cups a day, so she needs about 0.56% of the market. Then ask: is that realistic for one shop?
   How many cups can one counter serve in an hour? A good candidate turns the number into a judgement.

### Try 3 questions (solutions open)

**1. (Consulting math, break-even)** A tiffin service has fixed costs of Rs 2,00,000 a month. It sells meals at Rs 50 and each meal costs Rs 30 to make. How many meals a month to break even?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Margin per meal = 50 - 30 = Rs 20. Break-even = 2,00,000 / 20 = **10,000 meals a month** (about 333 a day for 30 days).

</details>

**2. (Consulting guesstimate)** Estimate the number of UPI payments made at a college canteen in one day.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

One approach: 2,000 students; 60% visit the canteen daily = 1,200 visitors; on average 1.5 purchases each = 1,800 purchases; 70% paid by UPI = **about 1,260 UPI payments a day**. Sanity check: if the canteen is open 8 hours, that is about 2-3 payments a minute, which is believable at a busy counter.

</details>

**3. Twin of a commonly reported FMCG management-trainee case (sales decline).** A soap brand's sales are falling in rural Andhra Pradesh. What would you check first?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Check in order: (1) Is the whole category falling or just our brand (market share)? (2) Distribution: are shops stocked? (3) Price: did we or a competitor change price or pack size? (4) Product: any quality complaints? (5) Promotion: did a competitor run schemes? (6) Season or income effects (crop failure). Then focus on the biggest driver.

</details>

## Preview: The HR interview, weak vs strong answers

### The idea in plain words

An HR interview is like a family meeting before a wedding: nobody tests your maths, but everyone wants to know whether you are
genuine, steady and easy to live with. You can't fake that for long, so the plan is simple: **prepare true stories in
advance, and tell them well.**

**The pattern for every fresher answer**
1. **Proof, not adjectives.** One real example instead of "hardworking, quick learner, passionate".
2. **Your own part in it.** "I handled the database", not "we did everything".
3. **What changed, or what you learned.** No invented numbers.
4. **A link back to this job.**

Keep each answer to about **one minute**. Never invent experience: HR asks follow-ups, and background checks happen later.

### Solved examples

Examples use made-up students. Replace every detail with **your own** true facts.

**1. Tell me about yourself.**
- *Weak:* "My name is Ravi. My father is a farmer and my mother is a homemaker. I have one sister. I did my schooling in Ongole. My hobbies are cricket and music. I am hardworking and a team player."
- *Strong:* "I'm Ravi, a final-year CSE student at Narayana Engineering College. I enjoy building small web apps. In third year I built a canteen pre-order app with two friends; I wrote the backend, and about 200 students used it for a semester. That taught me to fix bugs fast when real users complain. I'd like to start my career in a team like yours, where I can learn to build software at scale."
- *Why:* present, past, future; a real proof; ends with this job. Family details only if asked.

**4. What is your greatest weakness?**
- *Weak:* "I am a perfectionist" or "I have no weaknesses."
- *Strong:* "I used to get nervous speaking in front of groups. So I volunteered to present our project at the department review, and I practised with friends three times. I still get nervous, but I prepare notes and it goes fine now."
- *Why:* real, not central to the job, with action and progress. Never name a core skill of the role.

### Try 3 questions (solutions open)

Write your answer, time it, and say it aloud to a friend. Then compare with the model.

**1.** Turn this weak answer into a strong one: "I want to join your company because it is a big MNC with good salary."

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Model: "I read that your company runs large cloud projects for retail clients, and you train freshers in cloud basics. I did an AWS basics course and deployed my project on a free tier, so I'd like to grow in that area here."

</details>

**2.** You have two backlogs (both cleared). Write your answer in under 40 words.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Model: "In second year I had two backlogs because I didn't plan my time. I cleared both in the next attempt, and since then I haven't had any; my last three semesters improved."

</details>

**3.** What is wrong with this answer to "Why should we hire you?": "Because I am honest, hardworking and a quick learner"?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Three adjectives, no proof, nothing about the company's needs. Fix: their top need + your proof + why here.

</details>

## Preview: Percentages

### The idea in plain words

"Per cent" means **"out of 100"**. 25% means 25 out of every 100.

A real-life example: you pay ₹2,400 for groceries on a UPI app that gives **5% cashback**. 5% of 2,400 = 5/100 × 2,400 = ₹120.
But the offer says "maximum ₹100". So you get only ₹100. Reading the full condition is part of percentage questions too.

The most useful habit: **turn percentages into fractions.** 25% = 1/4, 20% = 1/5, 12.5% = 1/8. Fractions are faster to
calculate in your head than decimals.

### Solved examples

**Example 3 (medium). A salary is increased by 10% and then by 20%. What is the total increase?**

Net = 10 + 20 + (10 × 20)/100 = 30 + 2 = **32%**.
Check with ₹100: 100 → 110 → 132. Yes, 32%.

**Example 4 (medium). Anjali's salary is 25% more than Bhanu's. By what percent is Bhanu's salary less than Anjali's?**

Less by = 25/(100 + 25) × 100 = **20%**.
Check: Bhanu ₹100, Anjali ₹125. Difference 25 out of 125 = 20%.

### Try 3 questions (solutions open)

**1.** The price of sugar falls by 20%. By what percent can a family increase its use without spending more?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**25%.** 20/(100 − 20) × 100 = 25%.

</details>

**2.** A student needs 40% to pass. He gets 178 marks and fails by 22 marks. Find the maximum marks.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**500.** Pass mark = 178 + 22 = 200. 200 is 40% of max → max = 200/0.4 = 500.

</details>

**3.** Fresh grapes have 90% water. Dry grapes have 20% water. How many kg of dry grapes can you get from 20 kg of fresh grapes?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**2.5 kg.** Solid part of fresh grapes = 10% of 20 = 2 kg. In dry grapes, solid is 80%. So dry weight = 2/0.8 = 2.5 kg.

</details>

# College Placements: Campus Recruitment Training Programme

A complete, free-to-read placement course for **B.Tech, MCA and MBA** students: aptitude, verbal skills, technical skills, coding, interviews,
company-specific preparation, and how **AI** is changing hiring. It is built by **NeoTridhi** around the six training modules of a campus recruitment
training programme, in simple English, with solved examples, practice sets with answers, code you can run, and real placement scenarios.

> **How to read this:** open a module, read its `README.md` first, then the topic files in order. Try every practice set *before* you open the
> answers. Coding files are in each module's `code/` folder. PDFs for offline reading are in [`pdf/`](pdf/). Short video lessons (about 15 minutes each)
> follow the same order.

## The modules

| # | Module | What you will be able to do |
|---|---|---|

| 2 | [Verbal Ability & Communication Skills](02-verbal-ability-and-communication/) | Score in the verbal sections, write professional emails, speak with confidence |
| 3 | [Technical Training: Programming, Coding, Data Structures & DBMS](03-technical-training/) | Explain and use core CS concepts: programming, OOP, DSA, SQL, OS and networks |

| 5 | [Interview Preparation: Technical & HR, Group Discussions & Resume](05-interview-preparation/) | Build a one-page resume, handle technical and HR rounds and GDs |
| 6 | [Company-Specific Recruitment Preparation](06-company-specific-preparation/) | Know each top recruiter's process, test pattern and official practice links |
| 7 | [AI and the Future of Placements](07-ai-and-the-future-of-placements/) | Understand AI-era hiring, the skills in demand, and use AI honestly to prepare |

## A suggested 12-week plan

| Week | Focus | Modules |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Number system, percentages, ratio, averages; grammar basics; Python fundamentals | 1, 2, 3 |
| 3-4 | Time & work, speed & distance, P&C, probability; reading comprehension; OOP and complexity | 1, 2, 3 |
| 5-6 | Logical reasoning and data interpretation; email writing and speaking; data structures | 1, 2, 3 |
| 7-8 | Algorithms, SQL and DBMS, OS and networks; easy and medium coding problems | 3, 4 |
| 9 | Resume, LinkedIn and GitHub; HR questions; group discussions | 5 |
| 10 | Company-specific preparation for your target companies; mock tests | 4, 6 |
| 11 | Technical and HR mock interviews; AI skills and a small AI project | 5, 7 |
| 12 | Full mock tests under time, review of weak areas, final checklist | all |

**Every day:** 20 aptitude questions, 2 coding problems, 10 minutes of spoken English. **Every week:** one timed mock test.

## For B.Tech, MCA and MBA students
- **B.Tech (all branches):** all modules. Non-CS branches: Module 3 fundamentals + Module 7's branch-wise skills are especially important.
- **MCA:** all modules, with extra focus on Modules 3 and 4.
- **MBA:** Modules 1, 2, 5 (including the MBA personal interview and case basics), 6 (non-IT and MBA recruiters) and 7.

## Honesty notes
- Practice questions are **original**, written in the style of common placement tests. Company pages link to **official** sites and official
  practice tests where they exist.
- Test patterns and processes change every year. Each company page says when it was checked; **always confirm on the official page** before your test.
- Code samples were run before publishing; their real output is shown.
